A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Frequently
asked.

Are the vehicle prices live market prices?＋

No. All prices in this demonstration are illustrative estimates and are not verified live market prices.

Can I enquire about a vehicle?＋

Yes. Use the enquiry button on any vehicle page to create a local demo message. It is not sent to VELORE or a seller.

How do I list my car?＋

Visit Present your car and follow the guided listing form. Your draft is saved locally in your browser for demonstration.

Does VELORE facilitate payments or sales?＋

This frontend demonstration provides discovery and enquiry interfaces only. It does not process payments or complete transactions.
